Learn Chinese for Beginners · Display name: Learn Chinese

Product overview

Learn Chinese teaches beginner Mandarin with Simplified characters (Hanzi), Hanyu Pinyin, the four tones, everyday words, phrases, and quizzes. Learning data stays on the device. There is no account.

Supported Apple devices

  • iPhone
  • iPad
  • Apple Watch companion for a short synced word list

The Watch app shows Hanzi, Pinyin, and a translation. It does not speak.

How lessons and quizzes work

Open Characters for tones, initials, finals, and sandhi examples. Open Words or Phrases for category cards. Quiz uses the same curriculum (multiple choice, reverse choice, typing, and phrase order). Answers are based on the bundled pack, not a live server.

Mandarin vocabulary

Each word card shows Hanzi, Pinyin with tone marks, and a meaning in the translation language you choose in Settings. Favorites and “learned” marks are local.

Hanzi and Pinyin

Lessons use Simplified Chinese. Pinyin uses standard Hanyu Pinyin tone marks. Some two-syllable items are written in the usual concatenated style (for example 再见 → zàijiàn).

Four-tone pronunciation troubleshooting

The Characters screen shows mā / má / mǎ / mà, the neutral tone, and sandhi examples such as 你好, 一个, and 不是. The tone lines are teaching diagrams, not studio waveforms.

Audio and device TTS

This version does not ship studio-recorded Mandarin lesson files. Speech uses the zh-CN voice installed on your device.

  1. On iPhone or iPad open Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Chinese.
  2. Download a Mainland China voice (Tingting when listed).
  3. Return to Learn Chinese and tap the speaker on a card.
  4. Hong Kong or Taiwan voices are not the intended Mandarin voice for this course.

If nothing plays, confirm the device is not silent, and that a Chinese (China) voice finished downloading. The app never records your voice and does not score pronunciation.

Offline learning limitations

Curriculum, images, and interface sounds are bundled. Spoken Mandarin still depends on a voice the operating system can use. If no zh-CN voice is installed, cards and quizzes still work without sound.

Learning progress and favorites

Favorites and learned items live only on that device. Uninstalling the app removes them. There is no cloud backup.

Apple Watch

On iPhone open Learn Chinese → Apple Watch, choose up to 24 words or phrases, then sync. If the Watch shows “Set up on iPhone”, complete that step on the phone with the Watch nearby.

Installation troubleshooting

  • Update iOS, iPadOS, or watchOS, then reinstall from the App Store if the app will not open.
  • For Watch, pair the Watch in the Watch app, then open the companion once from the iPhone app.

Paid download

Learn Chinese is a paid app with no in-app purchases and no subscription in this build. There is no Restore button because there is nothing to restore inside the app. Purchase receipts and refunds are handled by Apple.
