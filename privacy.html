Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026
Product: Learn Chinese (Learn Chinese for Beginners)
iOS / iPadOS bundle ID: com.bekirsucikaran.learnchinese
watchOS bundle ID: com.bekirsucikaran.learnchinese.watchkitapp

1. Who we are

This policy describes the Learn Chinese app published by Bekir Sucikaran (GitHub: bekirs01). It applies to the iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch companion in the current shipping design.

Support is provided through GitHub Issues on the public legal site repository. See Support.

2. Application identification

Learn Chinese is an offline-first beginner Mandarin course. Lessons use Simplified Chinese characters, Hanyu Pinyin, and the four tones plus the neutral tone. The app does not require an account and does not include a developer-operated cloud service for learning content.

3. Information collected

The app does not collect personal information for analytics, advertising, accounts, or crash reporting implemented by the developer.

Verified in the current source:

  • No user accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics or advertising SDKs
  • No tracking (NSPrivacyTracking is false)
  • No collected data types listed in the Apple privacy manifest
  • No Firebase, Supabase, or other backend for lessons
  • No in-app purchases or StoreKit product catalog
  • No microphone, camera, location, or photo library permission

Apple’s App Privacy nutrition labels should match this: data is not collected by the app for tracking or developer-side profiling.

4. Local educational progress

Words you mark as learned, quiz mistakes, and related study state stay on the device using Apple UserDefaults / Flutter shared_preferences (privacy manifest reason CA92.1). This data is used only to run the app on that device. It is not uploaded to a developer server.

5. Local preferences and favorites

The same on-device store may hold:

  • Translation language for meanings and interface copy
  • Favorite words and phrases
  • Sound-effect and haptics switches
  • A short Apple Watch learning list you choose on iPhone

The Watch companion keeps a local cache of the items you synced so cards can appear when the iPhone is not nearby. WatchConnectivity exchanges that list between your paired devices. It is not a developer cloud account.

6. Audio and text-to-speech

This version does not bundle licensed studio recordings of Mandarin lessons. There is no assets/audio lesson pack in the app.

When you tap the speaker control, the app asks the operating system to speak Simplified Chinese with the voices installed on the device (flutter_tts, preferred locale zh-CN, Tingting when available). The app does not send your microphone audio anywhere. It does not operate a network text-to-speech service.

Short bundled sound effects (tap, correct, incorrect) play through audioplayers from files shipped with the app. They are interface sounds, not vocabulary recordings.

Apple or Google may process speech according to the voice you installed in system Settings. Enhanced or downloaded Mandarin voices are managed by the operating system, not by a developer server. The Apple Watch companion does not play pronunciation audio.

7. External services and third-party code

Runtime libraries include Flutter, shared_preferences, audioplayers, flutter_tts, and characters. They run on the device. They are not used here as analytics.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting these legal pages may cause GitHub to process standard web logs under GitHub’s own privacy terms. The mobile app does not load this website in order to function.

8. Apple purchases

Learn Chinese is intended as a paid App Store download. The current build has no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or Restore Purchases screen. If you buy the app from Apple, Apple processes the payment under Apple’s terms. The developer does not receive your full payment card details through this app.

9. Analytics and tracking

The app does not include analytics, advertising identifiers, or tracking domains. In-app privacy copy states that there is no analytics or tracking.

10. Data retention

On-device study data remains until you delete it in the app where that control exists, or until you delete the app. The developer does not retain a cloud copy of your progress.

11. Data removal

Delete the app from the iPhone, iPad, or Apple Watch to remove local progress, favorites, and Watch cache on that device. There is no cloud account to erase on a developer server.

12. Children’s privacy

The app is designed for a 4+ education audience and does not collect personal information from children. Do not enter a child’s contact details into GitHub Issues; describe the product problem only.

13. International considerations

Lesson content is stored in the app bundle. Learner translations (interface and meanings) also ship in the bundle. Because progress is local, the developer does not operate a cross-border student database. If you contact support on GitHub, GitHub may process that message on its infrastructure.

14. Security

The app does not transmit lesson progress to a developer API. Device storage uses the operating system’s ordinary app sandbox. Keep the device passcode enabled. No method is perfectly secure.

15. Policy changes

If a future version adds accounts, cloud sync, in-app purchases, analytics, or networked speech, this policy will be updated before that version is shipped, and the effective date will change.
